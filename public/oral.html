How it works

ORAL

ORAL is the bit that listens, does the work, and talks back. You say Alfred, report! once. He does not dump a wall of chat — he comes back with a short status you can trust.

  • You speak once — wake him; no endless prompt typing
  • He handles the busywork — inbox, follow-ups, ops checks, the boring stuff
  • He reports back — plain English: what is done, what still needs you
  • Proof required — no fake “done”; open items stay open until closed for real

Think chief of staff, not chatbot. He already knows the business, and he does not waste your time.
